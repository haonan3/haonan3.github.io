PhD Candidate NUS School of Computing · Singapore

I am a PhD candidate at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Kenji Kawaguchi. I previously did a PhD at UIUC with Prof. Jingrui He, and received my B.S. in CS & Statistics from UIUC, where I was mentored by Prof. Jiawei Han.

My research builds systems that reason, plan, and learn from interaction — spanning long-context modeling, world models, LLM reasoning, and autonomous agents. I am currently focused on RL and infrastructure for unified models, in close collaboration with MiroMind AI and Sea AI Lab.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to chat about research or collaborate.
