Haonan Wang / 王浩楠

PhD Student · NUS School of Computing

I am a PhD student at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Kenji Kawaguchi. Before NUS, I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Statistics from UIUC, where I was fortunate to be mentored by Prof. Jiawei Han and Prof. Jingrui He.

My research centers on building reasoning, agents, and generative models that learn efficiently from data and interaction. Recently I work closely with collaborators at MiroMind AI and Sea AI Lab.

Feel free to reach out if you’re interested in collaborating or chatting about research.
